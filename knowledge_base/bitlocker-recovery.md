# BitLocker Recovery Key Prompt

## Why it happens
Windows asks for the BitLocker recovery key after BIOS updates, hardware changes, or docking-station firmware changes.

## Steps
1. Note the Key ID shown on the recovery screen (first 8 characters).
2. On another device, sign in to the account portal and open Devices > View BitLocker keys, or contact the service desk.
3. The service desk verifies your identity and reads you the 48-digit recovery key that matches the Key ID.
4. Enter the key; Windows starts normally. Recovery keys are rotated automatically after use.

Never share the recovery key by email or chat.
