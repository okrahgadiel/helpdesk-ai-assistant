# Microsoft Teams Audio or Camera Not Working

## Steps
1. In Teams, open Settings > Devices and select the correct speaker, microphone, and camera.
2. Make the test call from Devices to confirm audio works.
3. Check operating system privacy settings: allow Teams to use the microphone and camera.
4. Close other apps that may hold the camera (Zoom, Webex, browser tabs).
5. Clear the Teams cache: quit Teams, delete the Teams cache folder, and restart.

## Headsets
Unplug and reconnect USB or Bluetooth headsets after Teams starts, and select them again in Devices.
