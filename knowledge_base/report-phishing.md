# Report a Phishing or Suspicious Email

## Steps
1. Do not click links, open attachments, or reply.
2. Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook. This sends the message to the security team and removes it from your inbox.
3. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, reset your password immediately and call the service desk — this is a security incident (priority 1).

## Signs of phishing
Urgent requests for payment or gift cards, mismatched sender domains, unexpected MFA prompts, and login pages that do not match the real company sign-in page.
