# Password Reset and Account Lockout

## Symptoms
User cannot sign in, sees "Your account has been locked" or "incorrect password", or the password has expired.

## Self-service reset
1. Go to the self-service password reset portal (SSPR) from any browser.
2. Enter your work email and complete the MFA verification.
3. Choose a new password: at least 14 characters, not one of your last 10 passwords.
4. Wait 5 minutes, then sign in again on every device (phone mail apps keep the old password and can re-lock the account).

## Account locked out
Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 10 failed attempts. Wait 15 minutes or contact the service desk to unlock after identity verification. Repeated lockouts usually come from an old password saved on a phone, mapped drive, or VPN client — update or remove it.
