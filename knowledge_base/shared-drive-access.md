# Request Access to a Shared Drive or Application

## How access works
Access is role-based and must be approved by the resource owner. The service desk cannot grant access without that approval.

## Steps
1. Open the service portal and choose "Request access".
2. Select the shared drive, Teams site, or application and the access level (read or modify).
3. Add a business justification. The request routes to your manager and then to the resource owner.
4. After approval, access is provisioned automatically within 1 hour. Sign out and back in to refresh group membership.

## Removing access
Managers receive quarterly access review emails and should remove access that is no longer needed.
